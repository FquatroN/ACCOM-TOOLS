# Reconciliation typography density

## Goal

Fit more information in the Reconciliation workbench and Eligible records area without reducing the legibility of actions, status badges, or the Current reconciliation panel.

## Approved change

- Reduce labels, selects, inputs, and headings inside the Reconciliation workbench by roughly 10–15%.
- Reduce the Eligible records table text and cell padding by roughly 10–15%.
- Keep action buttons, status badges, and the Current reconciliation card at their current readable size.
- Do not alter layout, data, interactions, or typography outside the Reconciliation view.

## Verification

- Inspect the workbench and Eligible records section at desktop width.
- Confirm all columns, status badges, and Add/Start actions remain readable.
